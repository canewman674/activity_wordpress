Exploring the WordPress Dashboard

Colin Newman 10/27/25
Section Purpose
Posts Used to create, edit, and manage blog posts or articles that appear on your sites blog page.
Media Stores and manages all uploaded images, videos, and other media files used throughout the site.
Pages Allows you to create and manage static pages such as About, Contact, or Services.
Comments Lets you review, approve, or delete comments left by visitors on your posts or pages.
Appearance Controls the design and layout of your site, including themes, widgets, and menus.
Plugins Manages extensions that add new features or functionality to your WordPress site.
Users Allows you to add, edit, or remove users and assign roles such as Administrator, Editor, or Subscriber.
Tools Provides options for importing, exporting, and managing various administrative utilities.
Settings Contains site-wide configuration options such as site title, timezone, and reading preferences.
